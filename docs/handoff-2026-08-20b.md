## THE HEADLINE FINDING: what a session is worth, per minute

Do not re-derive this. Measured over 900 simulated sessions per cell.

At the old flat 12-reversal budget (~40 trials, ~20 min for everyone), `fitThreshold` returned an
actual number on **99% of pitch sessions and 3–9% of lossy ones** — and the few lossy sessions that
did were **selected on having produced a narrow posterior**, which selects on the answer too:
pb4's survivors came back **0.54 ladder steps too sensitive** with nothing on screen to distinguish
them.

Two fixes, both measured:

1. **Report the band, never select** (PM ruling RT-90a b). Every session's posterior, quantised
   outward to ladder rungs; only the interpolated point is withheld. Bracketing coverage 92–96% on
   every ladder at the 80% tail, and 88–99% against a misspecified Weibull ear.
2. **Price the session per family** (PM ROI direction). Information per minute peaks at a different
   budget for each ladder:

```
ladder       reversals  trials  min  band%  pts/min   bias
pitch                8      27   14    78%      5.7   -0.12
timing              12      40   20    65%      3.2   -0.17
lossy/pb1           16      52   26    51%      1.9   +0.21
lossy/pb4           16      52   26    69%      2.6   -0.09
lossy/pb6           16      51   26    35%      1.3   -0.67   <- retired, RT-92a
```

Selection rule, stated before the numbers were read off it: *smallest budget whose bias is within
0.2 ladder steps, among those within 10% of that family's peak information-per-minute.* It gives
8 / 12 / 16 and it drops pb6 on its own.

**Pitch gets six minutes back and delivers more per minute. pb4's bias goes −0.54 → −0.09.**

## FALSIFIED — do not re-derive

1. **"A width-based stopping rule is the principled version of a session budget."** Built, measured,
   deleted. One flat window fired spuriously (pitch fell to 83% band while taking *longer*); two
   flat windows in a row never fired at all and ran every session to the 80-trial ceiling. **The
   reversal count is already an information criterion** — reversals only accumulate when the
   staircase is oscillating near threshold — and it beats interval-width sampled every 8 trials.
2. **"The inconclusive guard and the slope prior can be fixed to raise the answer rate."** Changing
   `BETA_MAX` 1.5→0.8 plus a one-bit guard gave 76–100% answer rates and looked excellent.
   **R4's pre-registered coverage assertion caught it**: a genuinely shallow listener (beta 0.7)
   dropped to 80.8% coverage against a 90% floor — the interval started lying. Coverage and answer
   rate trade off monotonically; only 1.5 clears the bar. **Both changes fully reverted.**
3. **"Reversing the ladder makes the staircase run away to the wrong end."** It does not. Reversing
   turns 2-down/1-up into an effective 2-up/1-down, which still converges — onto P=0.618 instead of
   0.707. The sampler looks fine; **the damage is entirely at the report**, where the two bounds are
   swapped and the sentence a person reads comes out exactly inverted.
4. **"`clipFor` only needs to be a file lookup."** 20 rendered timing clips on the two RT-75a-excluded
   windows were freely reachable, silently undoing the exclusion.

## Architecture the next session must not break

- **`sessionMinutes(family)` is the ONLY definition of session length.** It was written down three
  times (reversal budget, an `ESTIMATED_MINUTES` table, a `MEDIAN_TRIALS` lookup with its own
  arithmetic). `FAMILY_BUDGET.medianTrials` is a MEASURED constant and the test **re-measures it**
  rather than trusting it — that check is what stops it becoming another `MEASURED_LOSSY_FLOOR_KBPS`.
- **`startSession` / `nextTrial` / `answer` / `sessionResult` is the only sanctioned way to run a
  session.** It sources windows from `sessionInstances`, never a list.
- **`flipAxis` is the one place the inverted lossy axis is handled.** Any new consumer reading
  magnitudes must go through it.
- **Result copy lives in `src/content/staircase/`** and its fixtures are GENERATED from real
  sessions. Hand-written fixtures would not have hit the branch that carried a false sentence.
- **`eligibleSources(family)` returns the SHIPPING set**; `eligibleSources(family, true)` includes
  retired sources and is for the Lab and pipeline only.
- **The Lab limits page renders manifest statements verbatim.** Do not re-word them here.
