## Entry 001 — AI-music-creator wedge (PM proposal, 2026-07-17)
**Raw idea:** help creators using generative-audio tools (Suno etc.) understand music vocabulary and
prompt better, esp. for game/video/film audio.
**Seminar analysis:** as proposed, it's a different product (prompting education) — **no memo citation**
(fails D1: we evaluate and cultivate taste; we don't teach tool operation). BUT it contains a real
insight worth keeping: **AI-music creators are a natural audience for the existing gym** — they need
trained ears (delicacy: hearing artifacts/quality differences) and precise descriptive vocabulary.
**Salvageable forms (each WITH a citation):**
- (a) *Marketing wedge, zero build:* position the delicacy battery to creator communities — "can you
  hear what's wrong with your generation?" Serves N1 (niche brand) + C2. Cheapest, strongest.
- (b) *Future instrument — Articulation:* a performance task where users describe what they hear and
  are scored on discrimination-relevant precision. Serves D2 (performance measurement) and extends the
  Hume battery (delicacy verbalized). Genuinely novel; large build; post-battery roadmap item.
- (c) *Prompt-coaching feature:* no citation under the current memo. Requires a memo amendment to build.
