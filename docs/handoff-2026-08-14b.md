## TWO THINGS THE LAST HANDOFF GOT WRONG. Do not re-inherit them.

**1. "Nothing deployed" was false.** `https://vibe-check-app-sepia.vercel.app` is live,
public, current, and linked from the repo's About panel — it is the first thing anyone
clicks. Both instruments work, `/lab` is up. Plan accordingly; a whole session was
prepared to think the artifact was invisible.

**2. "Pitch drift's measure goes blind above ~100 cents" was a misdiagnosis.** Pitch
drift renders with `rubberband=pitch`, which is duration-exact. There is no misalignment
to be blind to, and reported drift stays at 2–3 ms. What collapses is the ENVELOPE
correlation, because a semitone of rubberband shift smears transients and changes the
envelope's shape. `temporalDrift`'s confidence is a validity signal for the **timing**
family and a **side effect of the manipulation** for pitch. Session E was about to design
around a constraint that does not exist.

## E1 — done. The cents ruler.

`pitchShiftCents()` in `scripts/clip-pipeline/spectral.mjs`. A shift of c cents is a
translation of the spectrum along a log-frequency axis; it resamples each frame's
spectrum onto a constant-cents grid, cross-correlates, and reads the peak with parabolic
interpolation.

**Recovery, synthetic (`scripts/clip-pipeline/pitch.test.ts`, 18 tests):** 13 levels from
0–200 cents within 2 cents · correct sign · ~0 on identical input · strictly monotone
across levels **3 cents apart** · stable 110–880 Hz · unmoved by a 20 ms timing offset.

**Recovery, real pool.** The renderer ramps segment-wise over 10 segments, so a clip
spans 5%–95% of its rung: prediction p95 = 0.95 × param.

```
  id    rung  predicted   p95|c|   conf%   err
  d1      25      23.8     24.1    100%   +0.4
  d10     25      23.8     24.4    100%   +0.6
  d4      50      47.5     47.7    100%   +0.2
  d16     50      47.5     47.6    100%   +0.1
  d13    100      95.0     94.9    100%   -0.1
  d7     100      95.0     95.0    100%   +0.0
```

**100% confident on every clip, including the two the old measure read at 19% and 66%.**

**NOT WIRED IN.** `validate` still grades pitch-drift on the anchor ratio. Wiring it is
E2's first task, because it changes verdicts.

**The real-pool table came from a scratchpad script and is not regenerable from the repo.**
