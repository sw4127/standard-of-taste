   never reaches the top five, even at 30 TRY / 70 FUND. The registry and the refusals, first under
   the old CRAFT/LEGIBILITY rule, score 1.5 of 5. The rule decided the list more than the scores did.

   production build. Before `2ce29cd`, "Generate" sat at 902 px on a phone, below an 812 px fold.

   into Tessavox" called `router.push`, Next replaced the URL back within 57 ms, and later pushes
   did nothing. It reproduced three times, and on the build before any of today's changes. The cause
   inside Next was NOT found; `20aca31` removes the router navigation from step changes, and the
   failing sequence now works. Seen only in the browser pane.
4. **Guard gaps found and closed:** the PRD citation parser gave every symbol on a line to the
   line's first file; `prd-scoring.test.ts` broke on Windows line endings (`core.autocrlf=true` here);
   the BA-10 no-model guard never walked `opengraph-image`, `icon`, `sitemap`, `robots`, `manifest` or
   `global-error`; one test passed only on this machine, because it borrowed a deliberately
   untracked document.
5. **The browser pane is unreliable as proof in two ways.** Its screenshots showed a stale frame
   that contradicted the DOM and hit-testing, and smooth scrolling stalls while the pane is hidden.
   Today's proofs rest on DOM geometry plus `elementFromPoint`. Capture pictures over the DevTools
