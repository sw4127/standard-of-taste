## The three defects found, all of which were live

1. **The delicacy share loop was dead.** The flow encodes 15 scored answers;
   the result page and card route decoded against the 18-item pool. Every
   completed session **threw** while building its own share URL
   (`missing response for "d7"`), and any share link that existed was
   undecodable. Fixed + regression-tested (`share-surfaces.test.ts`).
2. **`degrade.mjs` carried a stale rung table.** 6 of 9 shared labels
   disagreed with the shipped ladder; timing-smear and lossy-artifact were
   **off by a full rung**. `degrade --magnitude 2` would have rendered rung 3
   and written "magnitude 2" into the manifest beside it. Nothing downstream
   could catch it — every check compares audio to what was *rendered*, and the
   rung label is exactly what no measurement sees. Now one table
   (`scripts/clip-pipeline/rungs.mjs`), pinned by `rungs.test.ts`.
3. **d2 was 35.2% near-silent** (ceiling 20%) — the clip the PM found by ear.
   Layer A flagged it unprompted, the pool test went red, and it stayed red
   until replaced. Now 4.5%.

## Product decisions ruled this session

- **RT-41 — who this is for. RULED.** One product, three surfaces, one
  **non-monetary** gate. User of record: *the person who has to judge audio
  they didn't record* (AI-music creators first, aspiring producers second).
  Deliverable: a **per-flaw sensitivity threshold in physical units** + where
  your ear fails + a loop that moves it. Surfaces: **Floor** (assessment, free,
  no account) → **Gym** (training, account + **7-day per-family cooldown**,
  gated by retest validity rather than money) → **Lab** (`/lab`, credibility +
  resume artifact). **Anti-clone clause, on the record: we ship thresholds and
  item parameters; never a leaderboard, XP, streaks, or points.**
- **RT-42 — adaptive staircase approved**, pool expansion welcomed. Refinement
  that changes *which* expansion: a staircase needs a **deeper ladder** (8–12
  intensity rungs per family), not more replicates at the existing three.
  Replicates buy reliability for the fixed assessment (Spearman-Brown ≈ 44
  trials for α = 0.70); they are two different expansions with two different
  justifications.
- **RT-49 — revised session map approved.** Threshold instrument now outranks
  `/lab v2`.

### 2. Session E — the threshold instrument
Two measured facts that constrain the design:
- **Pitch-drift's temporal confidence collapses at the top rung**: 99/96/97/91%
  at rungs 2–3, but **66% (d13) and 19% (d7)** at rung 4. Expected — 100 cents
  of drift genuinely misaligns the signals — but it means the drift measure is
  **near-blind above ~100 cents**, and a staircase will walk straight into that.
- **The lossy ladder is wildly non-uniform**: d18 measures **94×** its anchor
  and d9 **31×**, against 3–9× everywhere else. It needs re-spacing, not just
  extending.
- Per-family reporting is **not yet honest**: 5 trials per family gives a ±40-pt
  95% interval. `byFamily` is computed in `src/engine/delicacy.ts:136` and
  correctly rendered nowhere. Do not surface it until the staircase lands.
