## THE VERCEL STORY, WHICH DROVE HALF THE SESSION

**The free tier hit 100% of 10 GB deployment storage.** Cause: every push to `main` is a production
deployment, and each stored **154 MB of instrument audio** — bytes identical to the previous push.

**Findings that must not be lost:**

- Vercel keeps **the last 20 production deployments in state Ready** regardless of policy or manual
  deletion (`/docs/deployment-retention`, "Exceptions to the retention policy"). That is a ~3 GB
  floor nobody can delete, and it is why deleting by hand appeared to do nothing.
- Hobby retention is **capped at 30 days** — a maximum, so something shorter is likely selectable,
  but the dropdown was never checked. **RT-Z6 is still open.**
- Bulk delete: `npx vercel remove <project> --safe --yes`. `--safe` skips ALIASED deployments, i.e.
  production. Do not drop it.
- An `ignoreCommand` build-skip was approved and then **rejected as unsafe**: the documented pattern
  inspects only the last commit, so under push-per-task a docs-only commit at the tip skips the
  build for the whole push and every source change in it silently never deploys.

**Fixed, permanently:** `public/` is now **35 KB** (was 268 MB). The audio lives at `audio/` in the
repository and is served from **jsDelivr pinned to commit `41ab6dd`**. Measured: `@commit` returns
`max-age=31536000, immutable`; `@branch` returns 12h and is mutable, so a branch pin would let the
audio change under a listener mid-session. **The PM confirmed the live site still plays after the
move.**
