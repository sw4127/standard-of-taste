'param' is the textbook floor and 'param+' the rate it would really have fired at: 5.4% at worst, over the advertised 5% on 1 of 4 ladders
and on the prestige test below. A floor that only sometimes keeps its promise is not a
floor, which is why the shipped one is read off the null distribution instead.
'stat' is how often the posterior median existed; 'point' how often the result screen
would have printed a threshold. The gap is the selection the arc avoids by comparing
the median rather than the printed number.

=== THE SHRINKAGE, STATED [SIMULATED] ===
R2 measured that a listener who IMPROVES moves toward the ladder floor and picks up its
truncation, so improvement is understated. This is that effect on the arc's own statistic:
ladder       true change    observed       shortfall
pitch           -7.19 steps    -6.92 steps -3.7%
timing          -7.88 steps    -7.87 steps -0.0%
lossy/pb1       -7.08 steps    -7.04 steps -0.6%
lossy/pb4       -7.13 steps    -7.44 steps +4.3%
A negative shortfall means the arc UNDERSTATES a real improvement. No estimator fixes
this; only a longer ladder does. It is a limit the copy must carry, not a bug to file.

=== PRESTIGE TEST — RETEST ON THE SAME CLIPS [SIMULATED] — 6000 persons ===
sigma 2.44 points of scale (e6's across-item figure was 2.58 — an upper bound)
floor 8.0 points of sway before the arc may speak (textbook MDC80 9.7)
held-out false positive 3.4% · power at twice the floor 98.3%
A sway that moves from +18% to +14% is INSIDE this floor and must read as no change.

=== DELICACY TRIALS — RETEST ON THE SAME PAIRS [SIMULATED] — 6000 persons ===
whole session (15 trials): sigma 11.2% of share · floor 40.0% = 6.0 of 15 items before the arc may speak
held-out false positive 2.2%

ASKING THREE TIMES: a person who did not change sees at least one family called "moved"
2.5% of the time — BELOW a single family's 5%, because the floor derived here
is conservative enough to absorb it. The multiplicity objection does not hold.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT LICENSE. Both reasons the Track H plan gave the PM for
RT-H2(a) are dead: the per-family arc is not impossible, and asking three times is not
what makes it unsafe. The surviving objection is coarseness — four of a family's five
items, 6 of 15 for the whole session — so the only movement it can ever
report is a near-total swing. RT-H2 goes back to the PM on the corrected numbers.
