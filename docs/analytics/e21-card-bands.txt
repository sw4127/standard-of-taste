THE REFUSAL RATE IS THE OTHER FINDING, and it is not a defect. Across listeners
drawn uniformly over a ladder's whole range, these ladders decline to print a
threshold for a large share of them — a listener at the very floor or ceiling
cannot be bracketed, and the instrument says so rather than guessing:
  pitch       point estimate in 80.3% of sittings
  timing      point estimate in 30.0% of sittings
  lossy/pb1   point estimate in 15.8% of sittings
  lossy/pb4   point estimate in 30.9% of sittings
So the card's commonest output on the thinner ladders is "not enough to tell",
and it must be built for that rather than treating it as an edge case.

WHAT THIS LICENSES. A family with one band gets no fineness claim on the card:
the threshold is printed and nothing is said about how fine it is. A family with
two gets one comparison, and that is the whole of what the card may assert about
how finely this listener discriminates on that axis.
